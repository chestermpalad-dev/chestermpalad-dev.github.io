A full-stack research defense management platform designed to centralize research documentation, defense collaboration, evaluation, revision tracking, similarity analysis, and AI-assisted voice processing.

Problem

Fragmented documents, verbal feedback, manual revision tracking, evaluation steps, and separate external tools.

Solution

One role-based platform connecting research workflows, defense documentation, evaluation, reporting, and integrations.

Architecture

Node.js/Express, MySQL, browser frontend, Python/WhisperX service, document tooling, and external APIs.

Engineering

Security

Role-based access, sessions, password hashing, secure file workflows, validation, and environment secrets.

Challenges

Real-time collaboration, transcription latency, NLP matching, asynchronous APIs, and multi-role data consistency.
